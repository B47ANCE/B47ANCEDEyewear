[h2]Overview[/h2]
Secure compatible glasses and supported hats with Lua-only retainers and finite improvised ties. No custom Java or JAR dependency.

[h2]Features[/h2]
• Native clothing GUID registrations resolve all tie and retainer definitions; unresolved new tie visuals are rejected before equip or material payment.
• All fourteen improvised materials use native head/clothing durability, with material-specific installed capacity for new ties.
• Right-click → Tie or Crafting → Tailoring → Tie worn eyewear consumes one material use and equips a condition-bearing tie linked to the same original eyewear. Its identity, condition, prescription and model are preserved. Monocles are excluded.
• Wear the eyewear and its matching tie together. Taking off either suspends protection; transfer both items together. Untie restores the original name and discards the matching tie, without refunding material.
• Paid legacy ties receive one finite tie item when worn. Lost or missing newer ties are not replaced automatically.
• Craft Cloth Eyewear Retainer or Eyewear Retainer in Tailoring and wear it in the separate retainer slot. Both now wear from native head/clothing damage; leather lasts longer on average by default. Take Off preserves the actual remaining item and condition.
• Find the non-craftable Unbreakable Eyewear Retainer in school lockers, optical-store containers and pharmacy cabinets. It remains unbreakable in ordinary use.
• Inspect remaining native durability, condition status and a live bar. Original black-cord, cloth and leather inventory icons and distinct 3D ground models are included. Distinct head-attached worn models have male/female variants. Worn fit, hair/hat/frame clipping and ground-model placement, scale and lighting remain NOT TESTED in PZ.
• All fourteen improvised materials share a visible neutral knotted-cord appearance, with separate male/female Eyes/Face rear cords and an under-chin Hat cord. The actual worn tie item owns its appearance; linked-target protection rules are unchanged. Actual fit/clipping and remote visuals remain NOT TESTED.
• Individual untied-eyewear retention profiles, supported straw/summer hat ties and optional Authentic Z eyewear support remain available.

[h2]Compatibility & Requirements[/h2]
Mod ID: B47ANCEDEyewear. Target: Build 42.21. SP, dedicated multiplayer and current optional-mod compatibility remain unverified. The server validates shared gameplay changes; clients submit intent and display replicated results.
New ties use three physical item types and reciprocal item-ID links. Back up worlds/players before upgrading; older packages do not understand these items or finite links. Reverting only the mod is not a validated save downgrade.

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
Server wear resistance defaults: improvised ties 1, cloth retainers 2, leather retainers 4 (integer 1–100). Each eligible vanilla head/clothing damage attempt has a one-in-N chance to remove one native condition point. Larger N lasts longer; material capacity is not a promise of that many hits; old ties and reusable retainers keep their ten-point scales. Setting changes do not refill condition. The rare item is exempt.
Server settings also control new tying, untied-eyewear fall multiplier, strapped-headwear fall value, wearable crafting/protection and retained-eyewear fall value (default 0). Worn finite items can still wear while protection is disabled. RareRetainerLootChance defaults to 0.25% per eligible new fill, range 0–5%; zero disables new spawns. Existing filled containers are unchanged.

[h2]Known Issues[/h2]
Durability uses native head/clothing damage, not the hidden successful knock-off roll. Native layering applies; a breaking blow can also dislodge the eyewear. Worn ties can wear even without their linked eyewear. Actual UI, combat, native condition synchronization, migration, transfers, save/reconnect and dedicated behavior remain unverified. Historical schema-2 records remain inactive. No automatic repair, free tie replacement, custom Java or post-drop re-equipping is included.

[b]Materials and reinforcement[/b]
Fourteen improvised materials, up to three matching thin strands, and one adhesive/duct-tape reinforcement. Existing damage is preserved. Zip ties require usable sharp scissors or a tagged knife to remove; tools are kept. New material capacities use native head/clothing wear, not knock-off counts. Original ten-point ties keep their condition: untie and retie to use reinforcement. Server sandbox durability multiplier controls newly installed contributions.

Current 0.15 gameplay: MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. SP, dedicated synchronization/persistence/restarts and detailed fit/UI acceptance remain untested. Stable release blocked.

[b]Crafted-retainer tape[/b]
Right-click an owned cloth/leather retainer in your main inventory to apply one adhesive or duct tape, worn or unworn. Defaults add +1/+2 native wear resistance without repairing its ten-point condition or replacing the actual item. A server sandbox multiplier snapshots new tape bonuses; the unbreakable retainer is excluded. Development MP testing build; current SP and dedicated gameplay remain NOT TESTED.
