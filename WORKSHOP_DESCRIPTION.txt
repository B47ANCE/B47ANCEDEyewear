Keep compatible glasses and hats from being knocked off. Tie them with common materials, or rely on the item-specific retention already built into the mod.

[h2]Tie and untie[/h2]
Wear supported eyewear or a straw/summer hat, then use the [b]Tie glasses[/b] crafting recipe or its inventory right-click menu. Choose twine, a rubber band, dental floss, or thread.

A tied item keeps its original instance, sprite, condition, and prescription data, gains the [b](Tied)[/b] name, and has a zero knock-off chance. Right-click a tied item to [b]Untie[/b]; no material is refunded.

[h2]Retention[/h2]
• Vanilla eyewear has individual untied knock-off values based on its frame or strap design.
• Supported helmets stay secured; loose headwear keeps its vanilla behavior.
• Monocles use a high knock-off value and cannot be tied.
• Supported vanilla eyewear, Authentic Z, and Faded's Authentic Z eyewear are included. Compatibility patches can register additional profiles.

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
Tie requests are validated by the server and synchronize the changed wearable. Native hosted and dedicated-server verification remains pending.
