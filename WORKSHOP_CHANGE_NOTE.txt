MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED

Adds one tape reinforcement to crafted cloth/leather retainers through their inventory context menu. Adhesive tape adds +1 native wear resistance by default; duct tape adds +2. Current condition, its ten-point maximum, actual item identity and worn mapping are preserved. Existing retainers work without recrafting.

The server validates target/tape ownership, state/revision, settings and one payment. Broken, already taped and unbreakable items cannot receive tape. New bonuses use a server sandbox multiplier. Improvised-tie reinforcement keeps its existing capacity rules.

Source-only; no upload or deployment performed. Current SP/dedicated gameplay NOT TESTED; G7 and stable G8 BLOCKED. Native head/clothing wear applies; no hidden knock-off counter or custom Java.
